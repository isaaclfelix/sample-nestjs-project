# Archipielago mission

How we operate as a project. Product shape and market position live in [vision.md](./vision.md).

## Human-first, AI-assisted

Archipielago is a **human-first, AI-assisted** initiative. Automation and agents may accelerate drafting, exploration, and mechanical work. They do not own the project.

**People** set goals, align objectives, make architectural calls, and have the last word. We are not aiming to become a fully automated, agentic-only project.

As the team grows, durable decisions move from individual judgment toward **collective ownership**: material changes should reach a clear level of consensus before they are accepted as project direction.

## Open source, developer-first

We are an **open source**, **developer-first** (developer-friendly) project. We exist to solve engineering problems and to pursue **objectively better** designs—clarity, correctness, testability, maintainability, and honest tradeoffs.

We do not optimize for:

- People-pleasing or blind agreement to satisfy non-engineering stakeholders at the expense of sound design
- Dirty bandaids or “ship now, invent the architecture later”
- Rushed delivery that undoes careful design or functionality
- Marketing-first pushes that defeat well-thought interfaces and behavior
- Artificial needs invented to look busy or trendy

When pressure conflicts with engineering quality, quality wins unless the maintainers explicitly accept a documented, time-boxed tradeoff.

## Diversity, inclusion, and contribution standards

We thrive on **diversity, inclusion, and non-discrimination**. Anyone may propose work.

Openness is not the same as accepting every contribution. Proposals and patches are judged against **guidelines and standards** (technical quality, fit with vision/roadmap, reviewability, licensing, and conduct). Rejection is based on **objective** reasons—scope, quality, conflict with stated principles—not personal attributes or identity.
